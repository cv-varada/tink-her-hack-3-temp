### Project Description
A portable carbon monoxide sensor for global health and pollution monitoring.

### The Problem statement
   With increasing amount of pollution in our everyday surroundings, one pollutant gas stands apart! Wait, wait,… not in a good way! Carbon monoxide is one of most poisonous and dangerous gas with an edge to become undetectable under normal scenarios. Plus an added property of difficulty in diagnosis of it’s poisoning. It is said that we human beings in 2025 has increased CO exposure than ever. Many of our lifestyle conditions are directly caused by this. Say migraines, hearing and visual impairment, attention deficiency, etc.. to name a few .It is often overlooked until and unless things are critical with diagnosis difficulty mentioned above. So early detection or a knowledge or data of our day to day CO exposure will not be a choice but a necessity in the near future.

   CASE STUDIES The recent rise in CO leakage affected deaths, tackling this is a major requirement. And for this, DETECTION is the key method! Deaths due to gas leakage in cars as passengers fell asleep while AC is on, using this as a suicide method, gas leakage from AC inside rooms causing the death of a whole family, landfills in Kochi causing severe air pollution, air pollution in Delhi and subsequent fall in temperature, Los Angeles wildfire 2025, etc… are a few case studies for the same.
   
### The Solution
Prevention is better than cure. Early detection, you’ll thank yourselves later! A portable CO detector is our solution for this problem. 

      Our portable CO sensor detects carbon monoxide gas in our surroundings making a buzzer sound with vibration in addition to LED light alert. It is made in Arduino. It also has a motor attached to it, mainly for vehicles, when leakage, the motor which is connected to the window automation of vehicle, works and opens the windows providing adequate ventilation. It’s gas sensor is fixed with a threshold value or a limit value, that which, when the level of detected gas is exceeded, our sensor works with alarm, sound and ventilation automation when provided.

      It is proven that adequate oxygen supply or ventilation does provide counter- action for CO- induced ailments or problems.

      The sensor uses a gas sensor which detects mainly CO along with other harmful gases like smoke, benzene, carbon dioxide, sulfur dioxide,ammonia etc.. to name a few.

      Our sensor detects pollution level around ourselves with providing knowledge about our day to day exposure about the same. It can enable us to react, respond and work according to the problem, thus, enabling public health and aide in pollution control activities.

It has many uses in different scenarios:

1. Detecting smoke and CO while travelling.
2. CO and smoke due to burning waste and cigarette smoking.
3. CO leakage in vehicles, rooms, open areas etc..
4. For leakage cars and vehicles, it also has a motor attached to it’s window automation system that works and opens the windows providing proper ventilation for the same.
5. This same motor system can be used for rooms as well.
6. Helps in diagnosis of CO caused illnesses. Makes doctor’s treatment better as it is identified early.
7. Portable like wrist watches, belt hangers, ear-phone case- sized etc…. 
8. We have buzzer, LED light alerts available.

Arduino code was first wrote on tinkercad and a working simulation of the product was designed. Variables were initialised and the components were connected to their respective pins. Based on the working simulation, a hardware project consisting of an Arduino and an MQ-135 gas sensor was built. LEDs were connected to the Arduino with the help of 220 ohm Resistors in series. An L293D Motor Driver was connected with a DC Motor to symbolise the working of a car window in real life.

![Final][Uploading WhatsApp Image 2025-01-26 at 10.20.13.jpeg…]()
 Power supply was given to the circuit through a USB cable after burning the code onto the Arduino. MQ-135 Gas Sensor was used to detect the presence of CO gas in the environment. LEDs were connected as an indication of smoke. A DC Motor attached to a wheel along with an L293D motor driver was connected to symbolise the working of a car window in real life. Hence the product in the presence of Carbon Monoxide gas produced an alarming red light and the wheel started to rotate enabling the automation of ventilation in automobile systems.
